Halfstop: A Field Atlas for Photographers

A field atlas for photographers. Scout locations, pin waypoints, and time sunrise, moonset, eclipses and aurora on maps that work with no signal.

Open the map How it works

Every good frame starts with being in the right place early.

Halfstop is a field atlas for photographers. Scout a location, drop a pin, and know exactly when the sun, the moon or the aurora will be where you need them. Federal and state land, topographic and aerial layers, all downloadable before you lose signal.

How it works

Time the light, not just the drive

Sunrise, sunset, blue hour, moonrise and moonset for any pin you drop. Add eclipse tracks and aurora forecasts, and golden hour stops being a guess.

Pin it now, shoot it later

Drop a waypoint the moment you spot something worth coming back for. Sort them into folders by trip, by season, or by the light they need.

Know whose land you are standing on

National forest, BLM, state land, wilderness and park boundaries drawn on the map, with forest road numbers and drone ceilings. Find out where you can legally set up before you drive four hours to find out you cannot.

See the ground before you get there

USGS topographic quads and aerial imagery, with hillshade and contours. Read the ridge line, find the pull-off, and work out whether that overlook actually faces the direction you need.

Check the sky, not just the forecast

Cloud cover, fog, snow and storm tracks over the ground you are heading for. A clear sunrise forecast twenty miles away is not the same as a clear sunrise here.

Works where the bars do not

Download what you need before you go. The map still knows where you are at the end of a forest road two hours from a cell tower.

The light will not wait for you.

Open the map, drop a pin on the place you have been meaning to shoot, and find out when to be there.

Where this is going

What is shipped, what is next, and what is further out.

  • Shipped A map-first field atlasTopographic, aerial and street basemaps with stackable overlays, and a shareable URL for any view.
  • Shipped Sun, moon and sky timingSunrise, sunset, blue hour, moonrise and moonset for any pin you drop, plus eclipse tracks and aurora forecasts.

  • Shipped Public land and access layersFederal and state boundaries, wilderness, forest road numbers and drone ceilings, drawn over the ground they govern.
  • Shipped Offline map regionsMark the ground you are heading for and download it. The map draws with no signal.

  • Shipped An iPhone appThe same atlas, installed, without a browser around it.
  • Shipped Client-side GPX / KML / KMZ parsingFiles are read and measured in your browser, never uploaded.
  • Next On the App StoreTestFlight first, so the people who will actually carry it can break it, then a public release.

  • Later Route drawing and editingSketch a route, snap it to the roads and trails underneath, and export it as GPX for whatever you navigate with.
