Privacy

What stays on your device, what we hold if you make an account, and what we do not collect at all.

In effect from 8 September 2026. Last changed 8 September 2026.

The short version

Halfstop does not run analytics, does not use advertising or tracking cookies, and does not sell anything about you to anybody. Most of what you make in it — your pins, your folders, your photographs, your downloaded maps — is stored on your own device and never leaves it. If you make an account, your folders and pins are also kept on a server so they can reach your other devices. That is the whole of it, and the rest of this page is the detail.

Who we are

Halfstop is made by Halfstop, LLC, an Indiana limited liability company. If you have a question about anything here, or want your data deleted, write to support@halfstop.app.

What stays on your device

Unless you sign in, everything you create in Halfstop lives in your browser or in the app on your phone, and we never receive it:

  • Folders, waypoints and tracks you save or import.
  • Photographs attached to a pin. The image itself is stored on the device and is never uploaded, even when you have an account — only its identifier is synced, so a picture taken on your phone will not appear on your laptop.
  • Downloaded map regions. The map tiles are held on the device.
  • Settings — theme, units, coordinate format, which layers you had switched on.
  • Files you open. A GPX, KML, KMZ or GeoJSON file you drop onto the map is read in the browser. It is not uploaded anywhere.

Clearing your browser's data, or deleting the app, removes all of it. Export a folder as GPX first if you want to keep it.

What we hold, if you make an account

An account is optional. Halfstop works without one. If you make one, we hold:

  • Your email address, so you can sign in and so we can send you a sign-in link.
  • Your folders and what is in them — the name and colour of each folder, and for every pin its coordinates, name, notes, icon and colour. Coordinates of places you have saved are location data about places you care about, so we treat them as personal to you.

That is all. We do not store your device's live position, a history of where you have been, your IP address as a record, or anything about how you use the app.

Accounts and folder storage run on Supabase, which hosts the database on our behalf. Rules on the database restrict every row to the account that created it.

Where you are

If you press the locate button, your browser or phone asks your permission and then gives Halfstop your position. It is used to draw the blue dot and to centre the map. It is not sent to us and it is not stored.

One thing worth understanding: to draw a map around you, the app has to ask a tile service for the squares of map covering that area. Those services therefore see roughly where the map is looking. That is how any online map works, and it is why the offline downloads exist — a region you have already downloaded is drawn from the device with no request at all.

Other services the app talks to

Halfstop draws on public mapping and forecast services. When the app requests something from one of them, that service necessarily receives the request — which includes the area of map being asked for and, as with any web request, your IP address. We do not send them anything about you beyond that, and none of them are told who you are.

  • Mapbox — searching for a place by name, naming the nearest town for a pin, the label fonts on the topographic map, and the mapping library itself.
  • Cloudflare — storage for the topographic map archive.
  • USGS, Esri, NOAA / the National Weather Service, the FAA, BLM, the US Forest Service and state agencies — the aerial imagery, topographic quads, public land boundaries, airspace and weather layers.
  • OpenStreetMap — the street basemap, served by community tile servers.
  • A Valhalla routing server — only when you ask a trip to draw its road route.
  • GitHub — hosts the website itself.
  • Supabase — accounts and folder sync, as above.

Each has its own privacy policy, and we do not control what they do with the requests they receive.

Cookies and tracking

There are none. No analytics, no advertising, no third-party tracking scripts, no cookie banner because there is nothing to consent to. If you sign in, a token is kept in your browser's own storage so you stay signed in; that is a functional necessity rather than tracking, and signing out removes it.

Deleting your account and your data

In the app: Settings → Account → Delete account. It removes your folders and pins from our database and deletes the account itself. It cannot be undone, so export anything you want to keep first.

You can also write to support@halfstop.app and we will do it for you.

Deleting the account does not touch what is on your device. Clearing the app's data or deleting the app does that.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to see what we hold about you, correct it, have it deleted, or receive a copy in a portable form. We will honour any of those, for anybody, wherever they live — write to support@halfstop.app.

A portable copy is available without asking: export any folder as GPX or GeoJSON from inside the app, at any time, with or without an account.

We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for advertising.

Children

Halfstop is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect anything from them. If you believe a child has made an account, write to us and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

If this changes, the date at the top changes with it. If a change means we start collecting something we did not collect before, we will say so plainly rather than quietly editing a paragraph.
