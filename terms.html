The maps are not authoritative — this part matters

Halfstop is for planning, not for navigation or for safety. The mapping, land boundaries, road data, airspace and weather all come from public agencies and community sources. Any of it can be out of date, incomplete, or simply wrong at the place you are standing.

Specifically, and not exhaustively:

  • A road on the map may not exist, may be gated, seasonal, washed out, or private. A route drawn between two points is an estimate, not a guarantee that the road is passable in your vehicle.
  • Land boundaries are approximate. Do not rely on this to decide whether you are legally allowed to be somewhere, to camp, or to enter. Check with the managing agency.
  • Airspace and drone ceilings are a convenience, not clearance. They are not a substitute for an official pre-flight check, and flying is your responsibility.
  • Weather and forecasts are forecasts. They change, and conditions on the ground can differ from the nearest station by a great deal.
  • Sun, moon and eclipse times are calculated and do not account for the terrain in front of you.

Carry a paper map and a compass where it matters, tell somebody where you are going, and use your own judgement in front of the actual ground.
